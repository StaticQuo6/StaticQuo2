LEGAL RIGHTS DURING PROTEST

1. RIGHT TO REMAIN SILENT
   - You are not required to answer questions
   - Say: "I am exercising my right to remain silent"
   - Ask for a lawyer immediately

2. RIGHT TO LEGAL COUNSEL
   - You have the right to an attorney
   - If you cannot afford one, one will be provided
   - Say: "I want to speak to my lawyer"

3. SEARCH AND SEIZURE
   - Police generally need a warrant to search
   - You may refuse consent to search
   - Say clearly: "I do not consent to a search"

4. DOCUMENTATION
   - You have the right to record police in public
   - Note officer badge numbers and names
   - Document injuries immediately with photographs

5. DURING DETENTION
   - Ask if you are free to leave
   - If not, ask on what charge you are being held
   - Provide your name and ID only (varies by jurisdiction)

6. AFTER DETENTION
   - Write down everything you remember as soon as possible
   - Get contact information of witnesses
   - Seek medical attention for any injuries
   - Contact legal support organization

7. PHONE CALLS
   - You are entitled to make a phone call
   - Memorize important phone numbers
   - Have a legal contact saved in your phone

Note: Laws vary by country and jurisdiction.
This is a general reference, not legal advice.
