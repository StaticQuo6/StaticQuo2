COMMUNICATION PROTOCOLS

1. MESSAGE PRIORITY
   - FLASH: Immediate life-threatening emergency
   - PRIORITY: Urgent but not life-threatening
   - ROUTINE: Normal communication
   - WELFARE: Check-in / status update

2. RADIO PROTOCOL
   - Listen before transmitting
   - Identify yourself: "This is [callsign]"
   - Speak clearly and slowly
   - Use phonetic alphabet for spelling
   - Acknowledge receipt: "Roger" or "Copy"
   - End with "Over" (expect reply) or "Out" (end)

3. PHONETIC ALPHABET
   A=Alpha, B=Bravo, C=Charlie, D=Delta
   E=Echo, F=Foxtrot, G=Golf, H=Hotel
   I=India, J=Juliet, K=Kilo, L=Lima
   M=Mike, N=November, O=Oscar, P=Papa
   Q=Quebec, R=Romeo, S=Sierra, T=Tango
   U=Uniform, V=Victor, W=Whiskey, X=X-ray
   Y=Yankee, Z=Zulu

4. SITUATION REPORTS (SITREP)
   - Unit/Team identifier
   - Current location (grid reference or landmark)
   - Status of personnel (OK, injured, missing)
   - Resources available
   - Threats observed
   - Actions taken

5. EMERGENCY SIGNALS
   - 3 whistle blasts / 3 flashes: Help needed
   - 2 whistle blasts / 2 flashes: Come here
   - 1 whistle blast / 1 flash: OK / Understood
   - Continuous blasts: Serious emergency

6. MESSAGE RELAY
   - If message is for another team, prefix: "Relay for [team]"
   - Confirm receipt before disconnecting
   - Repeat back critical information
