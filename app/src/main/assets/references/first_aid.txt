BASIC FIRST AID

Stay calm and assess the situation before acting.

1. CHECK RESPONSIVENESS
   - Tap shoulder, shout "Are you OK?"
   - If unresponsive, call for help immediately

2. AIRWAY
   - Tilt head back, lift chin
   - Look, listen, and feel for breathing (10 seconds)

3. BREATHING
   - If not breathing normally: start CPR
   - 30 chest compressions (rate 100-120/min, depth 5-6cm)
   - 2 rescue breaths
   - Continue 30:2 ratio

4. SEVERE BLEEDING
   - Apply direct pressure with clean cloth
   - Elevate injured limb if possible
   - Apply tourniquet only if life-threatening bleeding
   - Note time tourniquet applied

5. BURNS
   - Cool under running water 20 minutes
   - Cover with cling film or clean cloth
   - Do NOT apply ice, butter, or creams

6. FRACTURES
   - Immobilize the injured area
   - Splint with available materials
   - Do not realign bones

7. SHOCK
   - Lay person flat, elevate legs
   - Keep warm with blanket
   - Do not give food or drink

8. WHEN TO EVACUATE
   - Unconsciousness
   - Difficulty breathing
   - Uncontrolled bleeding
   - Severe chest or abdominal pain
   - Head injury with confusion
